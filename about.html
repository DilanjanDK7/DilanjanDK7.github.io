About Me

Computational Neuroscientist — bridging theoretical physics and clinical neuroimaging.

I'm Dilanjan Diyabalanage, a computational neuroscientist and researcher at Western University. My work spans theoretical physics (Loop Quantum Cosmology), clinical neuroimaging (EpiFlow, MT-WAN EEG pain decoding), and open-source pipelines for fMRI and brain data. I focus on scalable, reproducible methods and translating complex techniques into practical tools for the community.

Mission: Bridging the gap between theoretical physics and clinical neuroimaging through reproducible, high-performance pipelines.
